About Blue Heaven Farm Stay & Resort

A sanctuary where nature meets comfort.

Tucked just outside the bustling city of Jaipur, Blue Heaven Farm Stay & Resort is a beautiful and comfy place to unwind. The venue is 100% safe and secure, ensuring a worry-free experience for families and couples.

Guests have full access to the entire property—including the swimming pool, garden area, indoor and outdoor seating, games equipment, and private parking. You’ll enjoy complete privacy during your stay with no shared spaces.
